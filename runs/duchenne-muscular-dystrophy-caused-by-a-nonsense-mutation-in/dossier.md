# Therapeutic development dossier: Duchenne muscular dystrophy caused by a nonsense mutation in DMD exon 55

> **MECHANISM UNCERTAIN** - The upstream mechanism - dystrophin loss and its cascade - is solid enough to plan against, and one arm (genotype-agnostic HDAC inhibition) is actionable today on the strength of a positive randomised endpoint and an approval that does not depend on variant. But the exon-55-specific layer that decides every dystrophin-restoring strategy is unmeasured: NMD efficiency for this PTC, and readthrough permissiveness of this codon context. Both gate readthrough and, via template availability, multi-exon skipping as well. They are measurable in roughly 6-10 weeks in one patient-derived cell line, on one shared plate that simultaneously answers the givinostat-combination question and banks the sup-tRNA mechanism result. The responsible plan is therefore: start the approved brake now, build the cell line, run the shared gating plate, and defer every capital-intensive commitment until those numbers exist.

*Decision-support output from an automated multi-agent analysis. Claims are labelled EVIDENCE / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS. Not medical advice; replaces no experimental validation.*

## 1. Disease mechanism

**Causal defect:** Hemizygous loss-of-function point mutation in DMD (Xp21.2) creating a premature termination codon in exon 55, an exon in the distal part of the central rod/spectrin-repeat domain. Nonsense variants are a well-documented, recurrent DMD mutation class (~10% of all DMD alleles; 726 nonsense mutations in 7,149 catalogued), with CGA>TGA hypermutability.

**Molecular consequence:** Premature termination codon in DMD exon 55 causing nonsense-mediated decay and/or a C-terminally truncated Dp427m that cannot anchor the dystrophin-glycoprotein complex; functional dystrophin is effectively absent at the sarcolemma.

**Key cell types:** skeletal myofibers (Dp427m), cardiomyocytes, satellite cells / muscle stem cells, fibro-adipogenic progenitors, infiltrating macrophages, vascular smooth muscle, CNS neurons expressing Dp427c/Dp140 (exon 55 is upstream of the Dp71 promoter, so Dp71 is spared)

**Key pathology:**
- Contraction-induced sarcolemmal microtears with calcium influx and mitochondrial calcium overload
- Repeated necrosis-regeneration cycles leading to satellite cell exhaustion
- Chronic innate immune infiltration and TGF-beta-driven endomysial fibrosis with fatty replacement
- Functional ischemia from nNOS mislocalization
- Progressive dilated cardiomyopathy and respiratory muscle failure as the dominant causes of death

**Evidence strength:** Strong for the gene-disease relationship and for the dystrophin-deficiency cascade (large genotype-phenotype cohorts; decades of mdx/canine model work). Weak for anything specific to exon 55: I retrieved no exon-55-specific mechanistic or therapeutic literature within budget, so exon-55 claims are inference from the reading-frame rule and dystrophin domain architecture.

**Major unknowns:**
- Whether this specific exon 55 PTC triggers efficient NMD versus producing a stable truncated protein — this determines whether readthrough (e.g. ataluren-class) has a substrate at all and should be measured directly in patient myotubes or urine-derived cells
- Whether exon 55 is dispensable: skipping a single in-frame exon would not restore frame, so a multi-exon skip (or readthrough) strategy is required — the minimal skippable block around exon 55 and the functionality of the resulting dystrophin are untested
- Residual dystrophin level in this patient's muscle (any trace expression strongly modifies progression)
- Whether brain isoform involvement (Dp140 loss) contributes to the cognitive phenotype in this genotype stratum
- Degree of pre-existing fibrosis/fat replacement, which caps the benefit of any dystrophin-restoring approach

Hemizygous nonsense PTC in DMD exon 55. ## 2. Therapeutic leverage points

- Translation termination at the exon 55 PTC (eRF1/eRF3) - the most proximal druggable step short of editing, with human proof that readthrough generates dystrophin protein in nonsense DMD (EVIDENCE, supportive: PMID 24349052) and an existing oral, mutation-class-matched agent.
- Mutant DMD transcript abundance (NMD efficiency) - not a therapeutic target in itself but the single highest-information measurement in this program: it sets the ceiling for readthrough AND for multi-exon skipping, and it has never been measured for this allele (HYPOTHESIS, not_studied).
- Stop-codon context permissiveness, measurable in a dual-luciferase reporter carrying the patient's own exon 55 sequence (EVIDENCE, supportive: PMID 40156864) - converts an unanswerable clinical question into a weeks-long bench experiment.
- The fibro-adipogenic/TGF-beta remodelling program, which is genotype-agnostic and has one approved agent with a positive randomised functional endpoint (EVIDENCE, supportive: PMID 38508835; PMID 38967716) - the only arm that is actionable today irrespective of how the allele behaves.
- Preservation of the myofiber substrate: fibro-fatty replaced muscle cannot be rescued by any dystrophin-restoring therapy later, so the brake arm has option value beyond its own effect size (INFERENCE, supportive: PMID 27566866).

### Comparative judgment

The structure of this program is simpler than the six-way matrix suggests, because one measurement gates most of it. Mutant DMD transcript abundance in the patient's own cells determines whether readthrough has a substrate AND whether a multi-skip has a template; and the same cell line supports the codon-context reporter, the sup-tRNA mechanism test, the multiskip nucleofection, and the givinostat-interaction question. The dominant recommendation is therefore not a modality choice but a sequencing choice: build one patient-derived myogenic cell line and run one shared plate before committing to any arm.

Across arms, the two PURSUE verdicts are earned for opposite reasons and should both proceed. The small-molecule readthrough arm is the only dystrophin-restoring approach whose dominant failure mode can be measured cheaply, now, in the right cells, with an existing agent - that combination, not confidence in the drug's average efficacy, is what earns PURSUE. I am deliberately not treating the ataluren clinical record as strong: the supportive data are open-label, registry and subgroup (PMID 41199731), only 23/38 boys responded even by immunostaining (PMID 24349052), confirmatory randomised evidence was not retrieved, and European regulatory status has been contested. The allele-specific assay is worth running precisely because the class average is a poor guide to this patient. The givinostat arm earns PURSUE on different grounds: a positive randomised functional endpoint (PMID 38508835), approval irrespective of variant (PMID 38967716), human in-tissue target engagement (PMID 27566866), and complete independence from the allele question. It should start now and should not wait on any experiment; early initiation matters because fibro-fatty replacement caps benefit.

Within the readthrough hypothesis, modality ranking is unambiguous. Small molecule has an agent, demonstrated myofiber access and an adequate model. RNA (sup-tRNA) has the better mechanism - designable against this exact codon, tunable in a way a context-agnostic small molecule is not (PMID 40156864) - and the worse problem: no validated muscle- or cardiomyocyte-tropic delivery, with the only mature demonstration requiring bespoke pulmonary LNPs and local airway access (PMID 42658930). This is the textbook case where a better mechanism must not outrank missing delivery. Hence INVESTIGATE, scoped to the near-free myotube mechanism arm on the shared plate, with the LNP biodistribution screen explicitly deferred to the platform field. The oligonucleotide version of readthrough is rejected on mechanism: steric-block chemistry cannot force a release-factor event, and the only coherent contribution (raising template) is measured anyway by the small-molecule arm's Arm A.

The multiskip arm is the hardest call and the one where I most expect disagreement. Its product is the most credible of any arm - del45-55 carriers are mild Becker, and the deleted protein is demonstrably expressed (PMID 37625413) - and it would serve a large cohort (PMID 39596689). But its delivery precedent is deteriorating in real time: suvodirsen failed for lack of efficacy (NCT03907072) and PGN-EDO51, the peptide-conjugated chemistry this strategy depends on, was terminated and withdrawn (NCT06079736, NCT06833931). Set against approved single-exon PMOs achieving low single-digit percent dystrophin (PMID 28398005), an 11-oligo cocktail paying a multiplicative co-occupancy penalty is unlikely to clear a therapeutic bar, and it would be an unprecedented CMC object. I nonetheless fund the front end, because Step 0 (in silico enumeration of smaller frame-neutral blocks from the patient's transcript) costs days and could materially change the geometry, and Step 1 is asymmetrically informative: a poor fully-skipped fraction under saturating nucleofection caps the modality definitively, since in vivo exposure can only be lower. That is cheap, decisive, negative-biased evidence - exactly what should be bought early.

The protein arm is rejected because its dominant failure mode is not untested but already realised in humans twice at Phase 3 and once at Phase 2 (NCT04632940, NCT04371666, PMID 32522498), with the same antibody also failing Phase 3 in IPF (PMID 38762797, PMID 39114938), and because the proposed invalidating study runs in a model class that has already failed to predict those human outcomes - making a positive result weak and a negative merely confirmatory.

What would change my mind. On readthrough: severely depleted transcript (<10% of control) with baseline reporter readthrough would move the small-molecule arm from PURSUE to LOW PRIORITY and promote sup-tRNA from a mechanism curiosity to the lead restoration concept, delivery notwithstanding. On multiskip: a fully-skipped fraction above ~20-30% with a shallow partial-skip ladder, especially with fewer than 11 oligos from Step 0, would move it to PURSUE and justify delivery-chemistry partnering. On RNA: independent validation of a muscle- and cardiomyocyte-tropic systemic LNP by any group, for any cargo, would promote it immediately. On protein: a published combination multi-ligand blockade result with a contractile-force benefit, or any dataset showing meaningful antibody concentration in diaphragm and myocardium, would reopen it. On givinostat: a controlled signal in respiratory or cardiac endpoints would convert it from substrate-preserving adjunct to a primary arm.

### PTC-readthrough-restore-full-length - oligonucleotide - REJECT

*Hypothesis:* Because the only defect is a single premature termination codon in exon 55, forcing ribosomal readthrough of that codon would produce full-length, correctly folded Dp427m including the cysteine-rich and C-terminal domains, restoring the whole dystrophin-glycoprotein complex rather than a partial surrogate. This is the most proximal possible intervention short of gene editing.

*Target / process:* Translation termination at the exon 55 PTC (eukaryotic release factor complex eRF1/eRF3); secondarily nonsense-mediated decay (UPF1/SMG1) which limits available template; *desired perturbation:* Partial, selective suppression of termination at the PTC (gain of full-length dystrophin output); optional concomitant partial NMD inhibition to raise mutant transcript abundance

*Strategy:* Oligonucleotides cannot themselves force ribosomal readthrough. The only coherent oligonucleotide contribution is adjuvant: raise mutant DMD transcript abundance by blunting nonsense-mediated decay so a readthrough agent (ataluren-class small molecule, or an engineered suppressor tRNA, which is an RNA not an oligo modality) has more template. Two sub-strategies: (a) sequence-specific NMD evasion using a steric-block ASO over the exon 55 PTC region / downstream exon-junction-complex deposition site, which is largely conceptual in DMD; (b) systemic ASO knockdown of UPF1/SMG1, which is non-selective and raises genome-wide NMD-substrate burden. Neither is a credible standalone therapy; (a) is at best a tool compound to measure whether template abundance is the limiting variable.

*Verdict rationale:* This is the clearest reject in the set, and the upstream analysis essentially says so itself by concluding NO EXISTING AGENT IDENTIFIED and by describing the realistic best case as a tool compound rather than a drug. The proposed experiment is not useless science, but it does not reduce uncertainty that is not already being reduced: the UPF1/SMG1 knockdown ceiling test is a mechanistic add-on to the small-molecule arm, and the actual decision-relevant number - transcript abundance - is produced by Arm A of the small-molecule package regardless. Funding this as a separate arm would double-pay for one measurement. Keep UPF1/SMG1 knockdown as an optional well on the small-molecule plate if a template ceiling needs defining; do not stand up an oligonucleotide program for this hypothesis. Note this verdict concerns the readthrough hypothesis only and says nothing about oligonucleotides for the multiskip hypothesis, which is a genuinely different proposition.

**NO EXISTING AGENT IDENTIFIED**

(EVIDENCE, ClinicalTrials.gov); constraint: For this hypothesis the constraint is conceptual before it is pharmacokinetic: an NMD-modulating ASO does not restore dystrophin on its own, so it can only be a potentiator whose benefit is gated by a separate readthrough agent.

*Biggest unknown:* Whether the exon 55 mutant transcript is actually NMD-limited. If patient myotubes show near-normal DMD mRNA, an NMD-modulating oligonucleotide has no job at all and the whole oligonucleotide contribution to this hypothesis collapses.

*Model fitness (partial):* Same patient-derived myotube system as the small-molecule arm, used as a tool-compound testbed: steric-block 2'-MOE/PMO ASOs tiled across the exon 55 PTC region and the downstream exon-junction-complex deposition site, with siRNA/ASO knockdown of UPF1 or SMG1 as the mechanistic positive control.. Mechanism: Partial only, and the mismatch is conceptual rather than technical. The model can measure whether raising mutant transcript abundance increases dystrophin output when combined with a readthrough agent. It cannot test a standalone oligonucleotide therapy because no oligonucleotide chemistry can force ribosomal readthrough; readthrough is a release-factor event (INFERENCE, contradictory, from mechanism described in PMID 40156864). Cell type: Partial. Correct lineage for Dp427m; cardiomyocytes, where the approved DMD oligonucleotides essentially fail to distribute, are not represented, and that is precisely the tissue where this modality's ceiling is lowest (EVIDENCE, contradictory: NCT03907072 suvodirsen Phase 2/3 failure for lack of efficacy). Phenotype: Mutant DMD transcript abundance (fold-increase over untreated), and dystrophin protein in the ASO+readthrough-agent combination versus readthrough agent alone. Secondary: genome-wide stabilisation of physiological NMD substrates by RNA-seq as an on-target liability readout. Limits: In vitro free uptake (gymnosis/transfection) massively overstates in vivo myofiber nuclear delivery; the model cannot address the actual gating problem, which is that a template-raising agent has no standalone benefit and would carry full systemic oligonucleotide toxicity. Nonspecific NMD suppression liability is only crudely captured in a dish.

*Best next experiment:* Run this strictly as a conditional, cheap add-on to the small-molecule gating experiment and only if Arm A shows the exon 55 transcript is substantially NMD-depleted. ### Downstream-degeneration-brake - protein - REJECT

*Hypothesis:* Much of the irreversible disability in DMD comes from the downstream consequences of dystrophin loss — necrosis, failed regeneration, TGF-beta-driven endomysial fibrosis and fatty replacement — not from the absence of dystrophin per se at any single moment. It also protects the tissue substrate on which any later dystrophin-restoring therapy depends.

*Target / process:* Class I/II HDACs and the fibro-adipogenic / TGF-beta-driven remodelling program in dystrophic muscle; broader inflammation and regeneration pathways; *desired perturbation:* Reduce fibrosis, necrosis and fatty replacement; increase the fraction of contractile muscle tissue and compensatory regeneration

*Strategy:* The node named in the hypothesis (class I/II HDACs) is nuclear and not addressable by a biologic, so a protein approach must substitute an extracellular node in the same fibro-adipogenic / regeneration program: ligand traps or neutralising antibodies against secreted profibrotic and anti-anabolic factors in the dystrophic muscle interstitium — anti-CTGF/CCN2 (pamrevlumab), anti-myostatin/activin (domagrozumab, apitegromab, taldefgrobep alfa), anti-TGF-beta, or anti-inflammatory cytokine blockade. (A) Patient-derived exon-55-nonsense myogenic cells - urine-derived or fibroblast-derived MyoD-transdifferentiated myotubes from this specific patient - for the drug-interaction/NMD question. (B) D2-mdx mouse with longitudinal diaphragm and cardiac endpoints for the mortality-compartment question.. Mechanism: Model A: EVIDENCE-adjacent and directly on point - it contains the actual exon 55 PTC and therefore reports faithfully on whether HDAC inhibition changes mutant DMD transcript abundance, NMD efficiency, or readthrough yield (the stated unstudied combination risk, HYPOTHESIS, not_studied). Model B: INFERENCE (supportive) - captures fibro-adipogenic remodelling, necrosis and regeneration, the processes givinostat is known to modify in human muscle (EVIDENCE, PMID 27566866), in the diaphragm and heart where human data are absent (INFERENCE, not_studied, PMID 38508835). Cell type: Model A captures the myofiber lineage carrying the patient's own allele, which is the only cell type in which the transcript-level interaction question is meaningful; it does NOT contain FAPs or macrophages, so it cannot address fibrosis. Model B captures FAPs, macrophages, satellite cells, myofibers and cardiomyocytes together. Neither model can answer whether slowing ambulatory decline translates to human survival - only long-term cardiorespiratory follow-up of treated patients can.

*Best next experiment:* Experiment 1 (primary, run first; weeks not months). Derive myogenic cells from this patient (urine-derived cells or dermal fibroblasts, MyoD-transdifferentiated) plus a non-DMD control and, if obtainable, a second DMD nonsense line. Read out DMD mRNA by ddPCR and full-length dystrophin by capillary western. Experiment 2 (confirmatory, parallel if resources allow). Experiment 2: diaphragm specific force and left-ventricular fractional shortening versus vehicle.).

- Question: Two gating questions, both cheap. (1) Does HDAC inhibition interfere with, or usefully potentiate, a future dystrophin-restoring readthrough arm by changing mutant DMD transcript level, NMD efficiency or readthrough yield in this patient's own cells? (2) Does HDAC inhibition improve diaphragm and cardiac function - the compartments that cause death - or only the ambulatory skeletal-muscle compartment in which it was validated?
- Supports if: Experiment 1: givinostat is neutral or increases mutant DMD transcript abundance and does not reduce readthrough-generated dystrophin - meaning the two arms are combinable, and the patient can stay on the approved brake while readthrough is pursued. - Decision enabled: Experiment 1 is the highest-value next action for this patient overall, because it is simultaneously the readthrough gating assay and the combination-safety assay; its result determines whether the downstream brake and a dystrophin-restoring arm are run together or in sequence, and whether readthrough has any substrate at all. ## 4. What is already known

- Human proof of concept exists that PTC readthrough generates dystrophin protein in nonsense-mutation DMD: ataluren increased dystrophin/spectrin immunostaining in 23/38 boys after 28 days of oral dosing (EVIDENCE, supportive: PMID 24349052). This is the single most decision-relevant prior result for this allele class, and it also shows the response is far from universal.
- Readthrough efficiency is dominated by stop-codon identity and immediately adjacent sequence context, and combinations with aminoglycosides (G418) or anticodon-edited aa-tRNAs can be additive (EVIDENCE, supportive: PMID 40156864). This converts the central uncertainty into a dual-luciferase reporter experiment and supplies a combination fallback.
- Clinical support for ataluren is open-label, registry and subgroup in character (e.g. ~3-year later loss of ambulation in 28 treated boys, all on corticosteroids; EVIDENCE, supportive but uncontrolled: PMID 41199731); confirmatory randomised evidence was not retrieved and European regulatory status has been contested (INFERENCE, inconclusive). Efficacy magnitude must be treated as unresolved.
- An approved, genotype-agnostic brake exists today: givinostat met a functional endpoint in a randomised double-blind Phase 3 (EVIDENCE: PMID 38508835), was approved in the USA in March 2024 for DMD aged 6+ irrespective of variant (EVIDENCE: PMID 38967716), improves muscle-tissue fraction and reduces fibrosis/necrosis/fat on paired human biopsy (EVIDENCE: PMID 27566866), and has >8-year safety data in 194 patients (EVIDENCE: PMID 40830818).
- That same biopsy study found histological improvement without concurrent functional improvement (EVIDENCE, contradictory: PMID 27566866) - a standing warning that target engagement in dystrophic muscle does not automatically become function.
- Secreted-ligand neutralisation as a degeneration brake has failed in humans: pamrevlumab (anti-CTGF) missed its primary endpoint in two Phase 3 trials, both terminated (EVIDENCE: NCT04632940, NCT04371666); domagrozumab (anti-myostatin) showed a 4-stair-climb difference of 0.27 s (95% CI -7.4 to 7.9; p=0.94) with no significant secondary endpoint (EVIDENCE: PMID 32522498). The same anti-CTGF antibody also failed Phase 3 in IPF (EVIDENCE: PMID 38762797, PMID 39114938).
- The exon 45-55 skip geometry is real and evaluable in vivo via a systemic PMO cocktail in mdx52 mice (EVIDENCE: PMID 30171548), the 45-55-deleted dystrophin is expressed and detectable in patient iPSCs across three mutations (EVIDENCE: PMID 37625413), and the strategy has broad cohort applicability (EVIDENCE: PMID 39596689).
- DMD oligonucleotide delivery remains unsolved and the precedent is worsening: approved single-exon PMOs yield low single-digit percent dystrophin with unclear functional benefit and negligible cardiac uptake (INFERENCE: PMID 28398005); suvodirsen failed Phase 2/3 for lack of efficacy (EVIDENCE: NCT03907072); peptide-conjugated PGN-EDO51 was terminated/withdrawn by its sponsor (EVIDENCE: NCT06079736, NCT06833931). BMN 351 (NCT06280209) and WVE-N531 (NCT04906460) remain active, so chronic paediatric dosing is at least procedurally established.
- Suppressor-tRNA readthrough is platform-validated but organ-specific: m1A-modified sup-tRNA in a tailored pulmonary LNP restored CFTR function in patient organoids and mouse lung (EVIDENCE: PMID 42658930). No DMD sup-tRNA clinical program was found (EVIDENCE, contradictory: ClinicalTrials.gov search, DMD + tRNA, upstream session).
- No exon-55-specific NMD, readthrough-amenability or multi-skip data were retrieved in this project (EVIDENCE, inconclusive: PubMed searches, upstream session). In an ultra-rare allele this is a literature gap and must not be read as evidence against any approach.

## 5. Critical gaps

- Mutant DMD transcript abundance and allele-specific NMD efficiency for this exon 55 PTC have never been measured. This is the master gate: it caps readthrough (no template, no protein) and equally caps multi-exon skipping (a degraded transcript cannot be re-spliced into a useful product). Everything downstream is premature until this number exists (HYPOTHESIS, not_studied).
- Readthrough permissiveness of this exact stop codon plus its flanking nucleotides is unknown, and context is the dominant determinant of readthrough efficiency (EVIDENCE: PMID 40156864). Without a patient-sequence reporter benchmarked against a known-responsive allele, any decision to treat or not treat is guesswork.
- No patient-derived myogenic cell line exists yet. This is a logistical gap, not a scientific one, but it blocks five of the six arms simultaneously and is the true rate-limiting step of the whole program.
- Whether a frame-neutral exon block smaller than 45-55 and containing exon 55 exists for this patient's transcript has not been computed. The answer changes the multiskip arm's oligo count, cost and regulatory burden by a large factor and costs days to obtain.
- Co-occupancy efficiency of a multi-ASO cocktail on a single nascent transcript is unquantified in human cells; the only cocktail data are mdx52 mouse (PMID 30171548). The fully-versus-partially-skipped transcript spectrum is the missing number.
- No DMD program has demonstrated meaningful drug exposure in diaphragm and myocardium - the compartments that determine survival - for any modality considered here. Benefit in ambulatory timed tests is being used as a proxy for an outcome it has never been shown to predict.
- Whether HDAC inhibition is additive, neutral or antagonistic toward a dystrophin-restoring arm (via effects on mutant transcript level, NMD or readthrough yield) is unstudied and is a concrete combination risk for this patient, not a generic caution (HYPOTHESIS, not_studied).
- Baseline degree of fibro-fatty replacement in this patient is unknown and caps the achievable benefit of every arm; it also determines how urgent early initiation of the approved brake is.
