# Evidence — DMD nonsense mutation within exon 55

## Candidates considered
| Role | Concept | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| LEAD | Skip exons 54-55 (2 oligos) | Smallest in-frame fix (-115 aa), in-phase 108-aa hybrid repeat, 3' endpoint inside the exon ≤55 mild zone, published ASOs for both exons, only reported coordinated-skip signal in the region. |
| BACKUP | Skip exons 52-55 (4 oligos) | Only product with in vivo rescue (Δ52-55 mouse: WT-like dystrophin, normal DGC, mild deficit at 52 weeks) and an exactly in-phase 103-aa hybrid; cost is four simultaneous skips. |
| BACKUP | Skip exons 55-56 (2 oligos) | Equally minimal and exon 56 is a natural MES acceptor, but no human exon-56 ASO retrieved, junction breaks R21 helix C, endpoint crosses beyond exon 55. |
| WATCH | Single exon-54 AO relying on 54+55 co-skip (1 oligo) | Best possible outcome — one oligo, lead product — but evidence is one unquantified sentence; run as an arm of the lead experiment, never as the plan. |
| WATCH | Connected-sequence single molecule with arms in exons 54 and 55 | Brogidirsen proves a two-sequence oligo is a viable single product clinically and regulatorily, but both its arms sit inside one exon; cross-exon linkage is unprecedented. |
| WATCH | Skip exons 49-55 or 50-55 (6-7 oligos) | Strongest human and clinical precedent via the 45-55 mild genotype and PBGENE-DMD, hinge-3 removal gives latest DCM onset, but 6-7 oligos is unjustifiable before 2-exon options are exhausted. |
| REJECT | Skip exons 54-57 (4 oligos) | Oversized 126-aa out-of-phase chimeric repeat, no human equivalent, and includes exon 57, documented as unskippable without double targeting. |
| REJECT | Skip exons 52-57 or 53-58 (6 oligos) | Six-exon penetrance problem with out-of-frame intermediates, unmeasured exon 56, hard exon 57, del58+59-class isoforms functionally inferior, outer-exon-only targeting reported to fail at this span. |
| REJECT | Skips of ≥8 exons (48-55, 55-62, 50-57, 47-55) | Larger deletion and more oligos with no functional gain over the 2-exon products. |
| REJECT | PTC readthrough (ataluren) | Mechanism-matched but regulators in both regions concluded effectiveness was not established; EU authorisation not renewed, US resubmission withdrawn Feb 2026. |
| REJECT | Cationic peptide-PMO (PPMO) delivery | SRP-5051 discontinued for prolonged hypomagnesaemia and eGFR decline; PGN-EDO51 stopped for insufficient dystrophin — uninvestable for a new exon. |
| WATCH | AAV9-U7snRNA dual-cassette vector | Only architecture natively carrying two antisense sequences with durable one-shot expression, but non-redosable, non-titratable and high CMC cost for a first-in-class two-exon skip. |
| WATCH | EEV-conjugated PMO (Entrada) | Most realistic independent delivery licensor with no exon-55 asset, but current human gain (+2.36% dystrophin) does not beat naked dual PMO; revisit on higher-dose data. |
| WATCH | Integrin αvβ6 peptide ligand (Arrowhead TRiM) | Muscle delivery shown for siRNA only, no nuclear splice-switching precedent and no DMD human data — architecture precedent, not a 2026-27 build option. |
| WATCH | Bipartite 5' splice-site decoy (5D-ASO) | Reported ~30-fold potency gain on exon 51 in NHP is the right rescue lever if the 54/55 pair underperforms, but untested on this exon block. |

## Products
**exons 54-55 (2 exons, 345 bp, deletes aa 2625-2739)** (likely functional) — Smallest frame-restoring product, only 115 aa lost, generates an in-phase hybrid spectrin repeat with a 3' endpoint inside the empirically mild exon <=55 zone, and needs the fewest oligos (2).  
Natural: No published Δ54-55 carrier found (searches: PubMed/EuropePMC/web for "exons 54-55 deletion", "del 54-55" dystrophin Becker; EuropePMC 503). Indirect: Kaspar group of deletions confined to exons 50-55 had median DCM onset 43 y (n=11) vs 29.5 y for exon 45-49 deletions; Fortunato review found mild/asymptomatic in-frame deletions never extend past exon 55, placing this 3' endpoint in the permissive zone.  
Domains: Deleted range 2625-2739 removes the C-terminal 62 aa of spectrin repeat 21 (UniProt 2580-2686) and the N-terminal 51 aa of repeat 22 (2689-2802). Junction fuses R21 aa 2580-2624 (45 aa) to R22 aa 2740-2802 (63 aa) = 108-aa hybrid repeat, matching the 103-114 aa native repeat length, i.e. an in-phase hybrid repeat rather than a fractional repeat. No hinge removed (H3 is exons 50/51); cysteine-rich, WW (3055-3088), and C-terminal domains and all of Dp71 untouched; actin-binding ABD1/ABD2 untouched.  
Rescue: None found for Δ54-55 specifically. Closest: Dmd Δ52-55 mouse (overlapping, same 3' endpoint) shows WT-level truncated dystrophin, correct sarcolemmal localisation and normal DGC assembly; Wilton/Fletcher panel showed every internal DMD exon including 54 and 55 can be skipped in vitro, efficiency variable.

**exons 55-56 (2 exons, 363 bp, deletes aa 2676-2796)** (plausible) — Equally small (121 aa) and roughly repeat-length-matched, but the junction breaks R21 helix C and the 3' endpoint crosses exon 55 into the zone where no asymptomatic human deletions are observed.  
Natural: No published Δ55-56 carrier found (same searches as above, plus "exons 55-56 deletion in-frame Becker"). Penalised by the Fortunato finding that no asymptomatic/very-mild in-frame deletion extends beyond exon 55, i.e. exon 56 falls outside the empirically permissive interval.  
Domains: Deleted 2676-2796 removes the last 11 aa of repeat 21 and 108 of the 114 aa of repeat 22. Junction leaves R21 aa 2580-2675 (96 aa) spliced to a 6-aa R22 stub (2797-2802) then repeat 23 (2808-2930): effectively a whole-repeat deletion with the break inside R21 helix C, giving a 102-aa truncated/hybrid repeat. Hinges, cysteine-rich, WW, C-term intact.  
Rescue: None found. No animal or patient-cell data for a Δ55-56 product.

**exons 52-55 (4 exons, 675 bp, deletes aa 2515-2739)** (likely functional) — Only in-frame product in this window with a direct, published in-vivo model showing near-normal muscle and cardiac phenotype plus a perfectly in-phase 103-aa hybrid repeat.  
Natural: No human Δ52-55 case series found; the mouse-model authors state "little is known about variants in... exons 53-55". Region-level support: exon 45-55 deletion is the best-characterised mild genotype (asymptomatic to Becker; ~90% dystrophin reported) and exons 48-51, 48-53, 49-51, 50-51, 51-52 deletions appear in fully asymptomatic adults >43 y.  
Domains: Deleted 2515-2739 removes the C-terminal 63 aa of repeat 20 (2475-2577), all of repeat 21, and the N-terminal 51 aa of repeat 22. Junction fuses R20 aa 2475-2514 (40 aa) to R22 aa 2740-2802 (63 aa) = 103-aa hybrid repeat, exactly the native R20 length - in-phase. Hinge 3 (exons 50/51) preserved; cysteine-rich/WW/C-terminal and Dp71 intact.  
Rescue: Dmd Δ52-55 CRISPR mouse: WT-like truncated dystrophin levels, normal sarcolemmal DGC, normal histology/echo and protection from exercise damage at 12 weeks; at 52 weeks reduced post-exercise grip strength and lower tetanic force at 75-125 Hz - i.e. functional but not fully normal with age.

**exons 54-57 (4 exons, 675 bp, deletes aa 2625-2849)** (uncertain) — No human equivalent, 3' endpoint past exon 55, and a 126-aa oversized chimeric repeat that fits the out-of-phase category associated with earlier cardiomyopathy.  
Natural: None found (searches for in-frame deletions spanning exons 54-57 / 56-57 returned no carrier reports). Exon 56-57 involvement lies outside the exon <=55 interval that contains all reported asymptomatic in-frame deletions.  
Domains: Deleted 2625-2849 removes the C-terminal half of repeat 21, all of repeat 22, and the N-terminal 42 aa of repeat 23 (2808-2930). Junction fuses R21 aa 2580-2624 (45 aa) to R23 aa 2850-2930 (81 aa) = 126-aa chimera, longer than any native repeat here (103-123) and ~108% of a repeat in relative phase - borderline out-of-phase/fractional. C-terminal domains intact.  
Rescue: None found.

**exons 52-57 (6 exons, 1005 bp, deletes aa 2515-2849)** (uncertain) — Acceptable junction phasing but no human or animal precedent, 6 oligos required, and the product crosses the exon-55 boundary beyond which no benign deletions are documented.  
Natural: None found. Same exon >55 penalty.  
Domains: Removes C-terminal 63 aa of repeat 20, all of repeats 21 and 22, N-terminal 42 aa of repeat 23. Junction fuses R20 aa 2475-2514 (40 aa) to R23 aa 2850-2930 (81 aa) = 121-aa hybrid, close to R22/R23 lengths, so phasing is acceptable, but three repeats are lost and the rod is shortened by 335 aa. Hinge 3, cysteine-rich, WW, C-terminal intact.  
Rescue: None found.

**exons 53-58 (6 exons, 1008 bp, deletes aa 2554-2889)** (uncertain) — Largest exon-count penalty among the small set with no human equivalent and a borderline-phased junction well past exon 55.  
Natural: None found. Extends two exons beyond the empirically permissive 3' limit.  
Domains: Deleted 2554-2889 removes the C-terminal 24 aa of repeat 20, all of repeats 21 and 22, and the N-terminal 82 aa of repeat 23. Junction fuses R20 aa 2475-2553 (79 aa) to R23 aa 2890-2930 (41 aa) = 120-aa chimera; relative phase ~111%, borderline. C-terminal/WW/Dp71 intact.  
Rescue: None found.

**exons 50-55 (6 exons, 1017 bp, deletes aa 2401-2739) - larger comparator** (likely functional) — Hinge-3-deleting products in the 45-55 class have the strongest human mild-phenotype and clinical-development support, and the latest cardiomyopathy onset, despite an un-phased 145-aa rod junction.  
Natural: No exact Δ50-55 series found, but it sits inside the exon 45-55 interval that is the best-documented mild genotype: del45-55 appears in asymptomatic men, isolated hyperCKaemia and mild weakness (6/22 asymptomatic cases; 18.6% of CK/WCK), with ~90% dystrophin reported, and deletions of exons 50-51 and 51-52 are asymptomatic. Caveat: in a 57-subject del45-55 cohort 28% had Becker phenotype, 19% isolated cardiomyopathy, and cognitive impairment in 90% of children - the latter tracks genomic breakpoints affecting the Dp140 promoter and is not expected from exon-level ASO skipping.  
Domains: Deleted 2401-2739 removes the C-terminal 23 aa of repeat 19 (2319-2423), the entire R19-R20 linker containing hinge 3 (~2424-2474, encoded by exons 50/51), all of repeats 20 and 21, and the N-terminal 51 aa of repeat 22. Junction fuses R19 aa 2319-2400 (82 aa) to R22 aa 2740-2802 (63 aa) = 145 aa, far longer than a native repeat, so no clean hybrid repeat; the loss of hinge 3 changes rod flexibility. Cysteine-rich, WW, C-terminal, Dp71 intact.  
Rescue: Product class validated clinically: PBGENE-DMD (dual ARCUS nuclease, single AAV) excises exons 45-55 to restore near-full-length dystrophin; first patient dosed August 2026 in Phase 1/2 FUNCTION-DMD in ambulatory boys aged 2-7 with exon 45-55 mutations. Multi-exon 45-55 ASO/PMO cocktails restore in-frame transcript and dystrophin in immortalised DMD patient myotubes.

**exons 49-55 (7 exons, 1119 bp, deletes aa 2367-2739) - larger comparator** (likely functional) — Best structural profile of any product - in-phase 111-aa hybrid repeat plus hinge-3 removal (latest DCM onset) - inside the clinically validated 45-55 window, but 373 aa lost and 7 oligos needed.  
Natural: No exact Δ49-55 series found; nested within the del45-55 mild genotype and adjacent to asymptomatic del48-53 and del49-51. Deletion of exon 48 alone is subclinical/very mild in 17 males from 9 families, and in-frame deletions around exon 48 are reported as attenuated, supporting tolerance of this rod segment.  
Domains: Deleted 2367-2739 removes the C-terminal 57 aa of repeat 19, hinge 3 (~2424-2474), all of repeats 20 and 21, and the N-terminal 51 aa of repeat 22. Junction fuses R19 aa 2319-2366 (48 aa) to R22 aa 2740-2802 (63 aa) = 111-aa hybrid repeat, within native range - in-phase, and hinge 3 removed. Cysteine-rich, WW, C-terminal, Dp71 intact.  
Rescue: Same class-level evidence as Δ50-55: PBGENE-DMD exon 45-55 excision in Phase 1/2 (first patient dosed Aug 2026) and 45-55 PMO cocktail restoration in patient-derived myotubes. No Δ49-55-specific rescue data.

Ranking: Rank: Δ54-55 > Δ52-55 > Δ49-55 > Δ50-55 > Δ55-56 > Δ52-57 > Δ54-57 ≈ Δ53-58. Δ54-55 wins on buildability (2 oligos; no coordinated 54/55 skipping reported, so assume 2) plus an in-phase 108-aa hybrid repeat and a 3' endpoint inside the exon ≤55 mild zone. Δ52-55 is the only product with in-vivo rescue data. Δ49-55/Δ50-55 have the strongest human and clinical precedent but need 6-7 oligos.

## ASO design evidence
**exons 54-55 (2 exons, 345 bp, del aa 2625-2739, junction ACAGAAACCAAG|GACCTCCAAGGT)**  
Published: Exon 55: Ex55_Ac0_30-mer PMO TCTTCCAAAGCAGCCTCTCGCTCACTCACC (acceptor-spanning) and hEx55_Ac83_25-mer GCAGTTGTTTCAGCTTCTGTAAGCC (Echigoya 2019, PMID 31416775/PMC6838919); Nippon Shinyaku/NCNP patent US20180142245A1 oligomers H55_8-28, H55_11-31, H55_14-34 (nt 1-34 from exon-55 5' end), 2'OMe-PS/morpholino ~21-mers, reported high-efficiency exon 55 skipping. Exon 54: Ex54_Ac42_30-mer GAGAAGTTTCAGGGCCAAGTCATTTGCCAC and hEx54_Ac22_25-mer GCCACATCTACATTTGTCTGCCACT (same paper). Wilton 2007 (PMID 17285139) built a panel covering every internal exon incl. 54 and 55; all targeted exons were removable though efficiencies differed.  
Coordinated: POSITIVE and unique to this block: 'directing an AO to human dystrophin exon 54 led to removal of both exons 54 + 55' (Wilton-group rational-design paper, PMC2835229). Stated in Discussion with no AO name, sequence, cell type or efficiency given; an earlier secondary web summary of the same claim said exon-54-only and 54+55 products arose in roughly equal amounts, which I could not confirm in the retrieved text. Supporting mechanism from the same paper: H53A(+39+69) gave both exon-53 and a substantial exon-53+54 skipped species in mouse cells. Searches returning nothing: PubMed 'single antisense oligonucleotide induced skipping of two adjacent exons dystrophin unexpected co-skipping' (0), 'dystrophin exon skipping ASO induced skipping of the adjacent exon in addition to the target exon' (0), 'dystrophin exon 53 exon 54 ASO skipping both exons cocktail screening' (0); Europe PMC HTTP 503 on all queries.  
Linked: Brogidirsen / NS-089/NCNP-02: single PMO with a 'connected-sequence' (dual-targeting) design joining two separate target sequences inside exon 44 to block two splicing regulators at once; highest skipping of the series, active in patient cells and in cynomolgus skeletal and cardiac muscle; in clinical phase 1/2 (NCT05996003). Aartsma-Rus 2006 (PMID 16753346): double targeting within one exon improved efficiency and rescued the previously 'unskippable' exons 47 and 57.  
Amenability: Exon 54 is the amenable member of the pair: one AO against it reportedly removes 54+55 (PMC2835229), and Ex54_Ac42_30-mer scored 62.0% predicted skipping. Exon 55 falls in the '5'Fast-3'Fast' flanking-intron class, and exons with a fast 5'-flanking intron are significantly less skippable; targeting nearer the exon 5'-end is more efficient (PMC10481881) - consistent with both independent exon-55 lead sets (H55 nt 1-34; Ex55_Ac0 acceptor-spanning) sitting at the 5' end. Endogenous multiple-exon-skipping in normal muscle uses donors at the 3' ends of exons 41/44/47/49 and acceptors at the 5' ends of exons 56/58/60/61 only (PMC5085753); neither an exon-54 donor nor an exon-55 acceptor is a natural re-join point, so there is no natural 54-55 bypass to recruit. No natural/endogenous exon-55 skipping rate was found. Critical frame risk: exon-54-only or exon-55-only products are both out of frame, so partial skipping yields no protein, not less protein.

**exons 55-56 (2 exons, 363 bp, del aa 2676-2796, junction CATTCATAAAAG|GTCCCATTTGGA)**  
Published: Exon 55: as above (Ex55_Ac0_30-mer, hEx55_Ac83_25-mer, H55_8-28/11-31/14-34). Exon 56: no human exon-56 ASO sequence or efficiency found. Wilton 2007 (PMID 17285139) states a panel was designed against every internal exon and every targeted exon could be removed, which implies an exon-56 AO exists in that panel, but I could not retrieve its name, sequence or efficiency (cell.com and ScienceDirect both returned 403). Li/Wilton 2020 (PMC7516190) skipped exons 56+57 in vivo, but in the mouse Dmd transcript and with one PMO per exon; sequences are supplementary-only and were not retrieved.  
Coordinated: None found. No report of one oligo skipping 55+56. Searches tried: web 'single antisense oligonucleotide induced skipping of exons 54 and 55 OR exons 55 and 56 dystrophin double skipping one oligo' (returned only cocktail/dual-targeting-within-one-exon work); web/PubMed '"exon 56" DMD antisense oligonucleotide skipping morpholino design nonsense exon 55'; PubMed '"double exon skipping" dystrophin antisense single oligonucleotide in-frame' (1 hit, theoretical applicability only); Europe PMC 503. Negative control from the closest paper: Li/Wilton 2020's exons 56+57 and 58+59 double skips each required a separate PMO per exon - 'No single PMO successfully skipped two exons simultaneously.'  
Linked: Same precedents as the 54-55 block: brogidirsen connected-sequence single ASO (two arms, one exon) and Aartsma-Rus double-targeting-within-one-exon. No precedent found for one linked molecule with arms in two different exons.  
Amenability: Exon 56 is the strongest natural re-join acceptor in this whole region: the 5' end of exon 56 is one of only four acceptor sites used in endogenous multiple-exon skipping of normal human muscle, and the exon 44-56 junction is the headline natural product (PMC5085753) - so the splicing machinery demonstrably tolerates exon 56 as the downstream partner of a long skip. Counterpoint: endogenous MES products run ~600-fold below full-length mRNA, so this is permissiveness, not a usable baseline. Exon 56 amenability to an ASO in the human transcript is unmeasured in what I retrieved. Genotype-phenotype correlation downstream of exon 55 is explicitly described as lacking because such deletions are rare, so the functional quality of a del55-56 dystrophin is less well anchored than del54-55 (PMC7516190). Exon 55 amenability as in the 54-55 block.

**exons 52-55 (4 exons, 675 bp, del aa 2515-2739, junction ATCAAGCAGAAG|GACCTCCAAGGT)**  
Published: All four exons have validated PMOs from the 45-55 cocktail work (PMID 31416775): Ex52_Ac24_30-mer GGTAATGAGTTCTTCCAACTGGGGACGCCT (90.1% predicted); Ex53_Ac9_30-mer GTTCTTGTACTTCATCCCACTGATTCTGAA (73.9%), Ex53_Ac26_30-mer CCTCCGGTTCTGAAGGTGTTCTTGTACTTC (75.2%), hEx53_Ac43_25-mer ATTCAACTGTTGCCTCCGGTTCTGA (7.3%); Ex54_Ac42_30-mer (62.0%); Ex55_Ac0_30-mer (120.4%). Exon 53 also has two approved clinical ASOs (golodirsen, viltolarsen) - chemistry/delivery precedent only, not an efficiency expectation for a 4-exon block.  
Coordinated: None found for any single oligo spanning this block. Relevant partial precedent only: Aartsma-Rus 2006 achieved multi-exon skipping across exons 45-51 with just TWO AONs aimed at the outer exons (45 and 51), but attempts to extend outer-exon-only targeting to longer stretches including exons 42-55 and 45-59 failed (PMID 16753346). The exon-54 single-AO 54+55 co-skip (PMC2835229) is the only intra-block coordinated event found and it covers 2 of the 4 exons. Searches tried: PubMed 'dystrophin exon 52 exon 55 two antisense oligonucleotides skip intervening exons 52-55 in-frame' (0); 'multiexon skipping targeting outer exons two antisense oligonucleotides dystrophin exons 45-51 intervening exons removed' (0); Europe PMC 503.  
Linked: Outer-exon-only two-AON multi-exon skipping (exons 45-51) is the only architecture precedent for covering intervening exons without targeting them (PMID 16753346); it broke down for longer stretches. Cocktail architecture is the established alternative: 10-11 PMOs for exons 45-55 in immortalized patient cells and in hDMD mice (PMID 31416775), and systemic Vivo-PMO cocktails in mdx52 (PMID 22869723, PMID 30171548). Brogidirsen shows a single molecule can carry two linked antisense arms.  
Amenability: Best-in-region per-exon numbers: exon 52 (90.1%) and exon 53 (75.2%) are the two most efficiently skipped exons of the block, exon 54 mid (62.0%), exon 55 the kinetically unfavourable one (fast 5' intron). Exon 52's 3' end is NOT one of the four endogenous MES donors (41/44/47/49) and exon 55's 5' end is not an endogenous acceptor, so this specific junction has no natural precedent (PMC5085753). Practical risk flagged by the cocktail papers themselves: PMO cocktails generate a ladder of intermediate transcripts, in-frame and out-of-frame, so a 4-exon block needs the full block to dominate to be productive.

**exons 54-57 (4 exons, 675 bp, del aa 2625-2849, junction ACAGAAACCAAG|GCCTTCAAGAGG)**  
Published: Exon 54 and 55 PMOs as above (Ex54_Ac42_30-mer; Ex55_Ac0_30-mer, H55_8-28/11-31/14-34). Exon 56 and 57: no human ASO sequences retrieved. Exon 57 is explicitly documented as having been 'thus far unskippable' until rescued by double targeting of two sites within the exon with 2'-O-methyl phosphorothioate AONs (PMID 16753346); that paper's exon-57 AON identifiers were not retrieved. Mouse-transcript PPMOs skipping exons 56+57 exist (PMC7516190, sequences supplementary, not retrieved).  
Coordinated: Partial positives at both ends but nothing spanning the block. (a) one AO to exon 54 removed 54+55 (PMC2835229); (b) exons 56+57 were skipped together in vivo but with one PPMO per exon (PMC7516190). No report of a single oligo producing 54-57. Searches tried: web '"Morpholino Oligomer-Induced Dystrophin Isoforms to Map the Functional Domains" exons 56 57 skipping single PMO' (confirmed cocktail, not single); PubMed co-skipping/double-skipping queries listed above (0 hits); Europe PMC 503.  
Linked: Exon 57 is the clearest case in the DMD literature where a two-arm/double-target approach converted an unskippable exon into a skippable one - the mechanistic rationale for the brogidirsen connected-sequence single molecule. If exon 57 must be included, a two-site strategy for exon 57 is near-mandatory.  
Amenability: Mixed-to-poor. Exon 54 good; exon 55 kinetically unfavourable; exon 56 unmeasured but a natural acceptor (PMC5085753); exon 57 is a documented hard exon needing double targeting (PMID 16753346) and is also in the '5'Fast-3'Slow' flanking-intron class, with 5'-fast introns predicting low skippability (PMC10481881). There is no endogenous donor at exon 53/3' and no endogenous acceptor at exon 58... (the natural acceptors nearest this junction are exons 56 and 58, not 58's neighbour), so the 54|58 junction has no natural precedent. Functional quality of a del54-57 protein is poorly anchored because deletions downstream of exon 55 are rare (PMC7516190), though the related in-frame del56-57 isoform stabilized beta-dystroglycan and gave milder pathology in vivo.

**exons 52-57 (6 exons, 1005 bp, del aa 2515-2849, junction ATCAAGCAGAAG|GCCTTCAAGAGG)**  
Published: Exons 52-55 covered by validated PMOs (Ex52_Ac24 90.1%, Ex53_Ac26 75.2%, Ex54_Ac42 62.0%, Ex55_Ac0 120.4%; PMID 31416775). Exons 56 and 57: no human ASO sequence/efficiency retrieved; exon 57 documented as requiring double targeting (PMID 16753346).  
Coordinated: None found. Only the sub-block events already cited (54+55 from one exon-54 AO; 56+57 from two PPMOs). Outer-exon-only targeting is specifically reported to fail at this length scale: attempts at exons 42-55 and 45-59 multi-skipping did not work (PMID 16753346). Searches as listed in the 52-55 and 54-57 blocks; Europe PMC 503 throughout.  
Linked: Cocktail precedent at this scale is real but is 10-11 separate oligos, not one linked molecule: mutation-tailored PMO sets removed 3, 8 or 10 exons in patient cells and skipped all 11 exons 45-55 in a humanized DMD mouse (PMID 31416775); long-term systemic Vivo-PMO cocktails in mdx52 (PMID 22869723; PMID 30171548). No linked multi-arm molecule spanning 6 exons found in any gene in my searches.  
Amenability: This block contains both the best (52, 53, 55-lead-dependent) and the worst (57) exons of the region plus one unmeasured exon (56). Six simultaneous 100%-penetrant skips per transcript is the governing problem: the published cocktails were explicitly described as yielding mixed in-frame and out-of-frame intermediate species, and partial skipping here is non-productive because single-exon intermediates are out of frame. Endogenous precedent is adjacent but not identical: natural MES joins exon 49 to exon 56, i.e. nature skips 50-55 (a block in your enumeration), not 52-57 (PMC5085753).

**exons 53-58 (6 exons, 1008 bp, del aa 2554-2889, junction TTACGGATCGAA|AGCTGCCTCCTG)**  
Published: Exons 53-55 have validated PMOs (Ex53_Ac9/Ac26, hEx53_Ac43; Ex54_Ac42; Ex55_Ac0, hEx55_Ac83 - PMID 31416775) plus clinical exon-53 ASOs golodirsen and viltolarsen (chemistry/delivery precedent only). Exon 53 ASO chemistry has also been explored as LNA/2'OMe and FRNA variants in human myoblasts (PMC10698779). Exons 56, 57, 58: no human ASO sequences retrieved; mouse PPMOs for 56+57 and 58+59 exist (PMC7516190).  
Coordinated: None found for a single oligo over this block. Sub-block positives: exon-54 AO giving 54+55 (PMC2835229); 56+57 and 58+59 as PPMO pairs in mouse (PMC7516190). Searches: all queries listed in the preceding blocks plus 'U7 snRNA multiple exon skipping dystrophin single vector tandem antisense sequences' (PubMed 0 hits); Europe PMC 503 on every attempt.  
Linked: Same as above: 10-11-oligo cocktails for 45-55; two-AON outer-exon targeting works only up to ~7 exons (45-51) and failed beyond; brogidirsen is the only clinical single-molecule two-arm ASO and both arms sit in one exon.  
Amenability: Attractive on natural-junction grounds: the 5' end of exon 58 is one of the four endogenous MES acceptors, so an exon-53-donor-to-exon-58-acceptor join uses a naturally used acceptor (PMC5085753) - but the matching endogenous donors are exons 41/44/47/49, not exon 53, so the exact 53|58 junction is still unprecedented naturally. Exon 53 is efficiently skipped and clinically de-risked as a target; exon 57 is the documented hard exon; exon 58 skipping in mouse produced a del58+59 isoform that performed worse functionally than del56+57 (PMC7516190), a caution for including 58. Six-exon penetrance problem identical to 52-57.

Non-transferable benchmarks: (1) Eteplirsen/golodirsen/viltolarsen/casimersen clinical dystrophin and skipping percentages are single-exon, single-oligo, naked-PMO numbers for exons 51/53/45; they say nothing about the yield of a 2-6 exon coordinated skip where every intermediate species is out of frame. (2) Brogidirsen's exon-44 skipping and dystrophin results are for a dual-arm oligo whose two arms sit inside ONE exon; it is architecture precedent only, not an efficiency expectation for arms placed in two different exons. (3) The 120.4% 'predicted skipping' for Ex55_Ac0_30-mer and all other numbers from the 45-55 cocktail paper are predictive-tool/in-vitro screen values, not protein-level efficacy. (4) Exons 45-55 cocktail efficacy in mdx52 and hDMD mice reflects a different, naturally-permissive junction (endogenous exon 44-56 joining) and does not transfer to 54-55, 55-56, 52-55, 54-57, 52-57 or 53-58. (5) mdx exon-23 PPMO potency, and the mouse 56+57 / 58+59 in vivo results, are mouse-transcript data at mouse splice sites; exon numbering coincidence does not imply human exon 56/57 amenability. (6) Endogenous MES abundance (~600-fold below full-length) is a permissiveness signal, not a therapeutic baseline.

Proposed designs:
- Lead: 2-PMO/PPMO cocktail for exons 54-55 - exon-54 arm at Ex54_Ac42 region, exon-55 arm at nt 11-31 (H55_11-31 / Ex55_Ac0), fewest exons, fewest aa lost.
- Single-oligo probe: re-test an exon-54-only AO (Ex54_Ac42 region, 25-31mer) for the reported 54+55 co-skip; accept only if 54-alone out-of-frame product is minor.
- Dual-arm connected-sequence single molecule (brogidirsen architecture): one PMO linking an exon-54 acceptor-region arm to an exon-55 5'-end arm.
- Fallback if exon 54 resists: 2-oligo exons 55-56 cocktail, exploiting exon 56's natural MES acceptor; needs de novo exon-56 ASO walk.

## Delivery platforms
**Unconjugated PMO (naked phosphorodiamidate morpholino), incl. dual-ASO design** — Charge-neutral PMO, 20-31mer, no ligand; uptake via dystrophic-membrane leak + fluid-phase endocytosis. Dual-targeting variant (brogidirsen) = two PMOs against two sites in the same exon, co-administered.  
Reach: Skeletal myofibers only at a low, highly variable level; diaphragm intermediate; cardiomyocytes essentially not reached at clinical doses (intact sarcolemma, no receptor route) - the key liability for a DMD55 program where LV function determines survival. Satellite cells poorly targeted by naked PMO.  
Human PD: Best naked baseline = brogidirsen (exon 44, dual ASO): dystrophin 10.27% and 15.79% of normal in dose-escalation cohorts, rising to 16.63% (40 mg/kg) and 24.47% (80 mg/kg) of normal at 24 weeks. Single-ASO approved drugs: viltolarsen 5.7-5.9% of normal at wk25 (from 0.3-0.6% baseline); golodirsen 1.019% at wk48 (from 0.095%); casimersen 1.7% vs 0.8% placebo at wk48; eteplirsen 0.93% at 180 weeks.  
Dose: IV weekly, 30 mg/kg (eteplirsen/golodirsen/casimersen), 80 mg/kg (viltolarsen, brogidirsen up to 80 mg/kg). Weekly infusion for life.  
Tox: Best-characterised safety of any class. Brogidirsen: no SAEs, no discontinuations to 80 mg/kg/24 wk. Approved PMOs: hypersensitivity/infusion reactions, rare proteinuria/renal tubular signals at high mg/kg; no magnesium wasting.  
Access: Fully buildable internally - PMO chemistry is commoditised, CDMO capacity exists, and the regulatory path (dystrophin as accelerated-approval surrogate) is established. Fastest route to clinic for an ultra-rare exon 55 cohort; dual-ASO 'connected-sequence' design is in the literature and reproducible.

**Anti-TfR1 Fab-PMO conjugate (Dyne FORCE; DYNE-251 / z-rostudirsen)** — Humanised anti-transferrin-receptor-1 Fab covalently linked to exon-51 PMO; receptor-mediated endocytosis into TfR1-high muscle.  
Reach: Skeletal myofibers, diaphragm and heart. NHP: 5 weekly 30 mg/kg doses gave 43% (heart), 52% (diaphragm), 18% (quadriceps) exon 51 skipping at 8 weeks. mdx FORCE-M23D 30 mg/kg: 77% of WT dystrophin by Western in heart, diaphragm and quadriceps with near-complete sarcolemmal localisation in heart and diaphragm. Satellite-cell compartment: PMO delivered to satellite cells and found in nuclei of newly regenerated fibres 3 months post-dose (preclinical, platform-level).  
Human PD: DELIVER (exon 51): 20 mg/kg Q4W mean absolute dystrophin 3.71% of normal (muscle-content-adjusted 8.72%), >10x the 0.3% reported for eteplirsen; Registry Expansion Cohort met primary endpoint at muscle-adjusted 5.46% of normal at 6 months; sustained functional improvement to 12-18 months.  
Dose: IV 20 mg/kg every 4 weeks (registrational dose/regimen); 546 doses administered at that level.  
Tox: Favourable across 54 participants: no new treatment-related SAEs; IRRs 11%, all mild/moderate, no dosing stoppages; headache 16%, nasopharyngitis 16%, vomiting 14%. No anaemia, thrombocytopenia or kidney injury; no clinically meaningful electrolyte (incl. magnesium) changes - i.e. none of the PPMO liabilities.  
Access: Dyne is independent; BLA for z-rostudirsen submitted May 2026 seeking accelerated approval with priority review, launch targeted Q1 2027, Phase 3 FORZETTO running. Platform access only by partnership/licence; an exon 55 asset would be a new conjugate on their established Fab and tox package - plausible co-development but they control the IP and are prioritising their own exon franchise.

**Anti-TfR1 mAb-PMO conjugate (Avidity AOC; AOC 1044 / del-zota)** — Full-length anti-TfR1 monoclonal antibody conjugated to exon-44 PMO (AOC). Longer circulating half-life than Fab format, supporting 6-weekly dosing.  
Reach: Highest measured human muscle PMO exposure of any class: ~200 nM PMO in skeletal muscle, up to 50x the concentrations reported for peptide-conjugated PMOs. Cardiac and smooth muscle targeted by design; preclinical atrial/ventricular exon 44 skipping ~7 skipped copies/ng cDNA. Human cardiac PD: none published.  
Human PD: EXPLORE44, 5 mg/kg: exon 44 skipping +37% absolute (7% to 44%, up to 66%); dystrophin +25% absolute over baseline (7% to 32% of normal; total dystrophin up to 54% of normal); CK reduced >80% from baseline to near-normal. n=10 in the 5 mg/kg cohort, 3 doses.  
Dose: IV 5 mg/kg every 6 weeks (10 mg/kg tested in healthy volunteers).  
Tox: Favourable; most TEAEs mild/moderate in DMD44 participants, well tolerated to 10 mg/kg in healthy volunteers. Note the AOC platform has carried serious safety events in other indications/payloads, so platform tox is payload- and dose-dependent, not a blanket clearance.  
Access: Hardest to access: Novartis completed the $12B Avidity acquisition on 27 Feb 2026; del-zota BLA accepted for priority review under accelerated approval (Sept 2026) with Phase 3 SAFARI44 as the confirmatory trial. An exon 55 program would compete for Novartis internal prioritisation; out-licensing the anti-TfR1 AOC format to a third party is unlikely. Treat as a benchmark and a validation of TfR1, not a realistic in-license.

**Cell-penetrating peptide-PMO conjugates (PPMO: Sarepta SRP-5051; PepGen EDO)** — Cationic/arginine-rich or proprietary 'enhanced delivery oligonucleotide' peptide covalently fused to PMO; adsorptive endocytosis.  
Reach: Reaches myofibre and (preclinically, e.g. Pip6a-PMO) cardiac muscle, but human exposure is roughly an order of magnitude below antibody conjugates.  
Human PD: PepGen PGN-EDO51 10 mg/kg: exon 51-skipped transcripts 4.26% but total dystrophin only 0.59% of normal - company concluded it could not dose high enough for meaningful benefit. SRP-5051 showed dystrophin increases but was discontinued.  
Dose: IV monthly (SRP-5051 30 mg/kg Q4W; PGN-EDO51 5-10 mg/kg Q4W).  
Tox: Class-defining failure mode: SRP-5051 caused hypomagnesaemia that was prolonged in some participants even after stopping, plus eGFR decline - development discontinued Nov 2024 on long-term safety/tolerability. PGN-EDO51 itself was well tolerated (all related AEs mild, no SAEs) but was stopped May 2025 for insufficient dystrophin; PepGen exited DMD entirely.  
Access: Available in principle (free IP space, several academic Pip/B-peptide series), but two independent clinical failures make it uninvestable for a new exon 55 asset. Deprioritise.

**Endosomal Escape Vehicle (EEV)-conjugated PMO (Entrada ENTR-601-44)** — Cyclic cell-penetrating peptide engineered for endosomal escape, conjugated to PMO; next-generation of the peptide class.  
Reach: Reaches skeletal muscle and gives measurable skipping in humans; no human cardiac PD.  
Human PD: ELEVATE-44-201 Cohort 1 (3 x 6 mg/kg IV, ~12 weeks): dystrophin +2.36% over a 4.00% baseline; exon 44 skipping +2.31% over 2.66%. An early functional (time-to-rise velocity) separation vs placebo was reported, ~3.5x MCID, on modest biomarker change.  
Dose: IV approximately every 4 weeks; dose escalation to 18 mg/kg planned in Cohort 3.  
Tox: Met primary safety objectives in Cohort 1. Program had been under FDA clinical hold, lifted 24 Feb 2025 allowing the adult MAD study; the hold history is a diligence item for the chemistry class.  
Access: Entrada is independent with exon 44/45/50/51 assets and no exon 55 program - the most realistic partner/licensor of a targeted-delivery chemistry for a new exon. But current human potency (2.4 percentage-point dystrophin gain) does not yet beat a naked dual-PMO, and higher-dose data are pending end-2026.

**Vectorised antisense: AAV9-U7snRNA** — Self-complementary AAV9 encoding modified U7 small nuclear RNA carrying the antisense sequence; one-time IV dose, continuous intranuclear antisense production.  
Reach: AAV9 tropism covers skeletal muscle, diaphragm and heart, and transduced myonuclei keep producing the antisense - the only class that addresses durability without chronic infusion. Does not transduce satellite cells durably, so dilution by regeneration remains.  
Human PD: Only human experience is the exon 2 duplication trial scAAV9.U7.ACCA (NCT04240314), systemic IV, results posted Sept 2025. No exon 55 or two-exon construct in humans. Preclinical dose-escalation has defined a therapeutic range for exon 2 skipping.  
Dose: Single IV dose; effectively non-redosable (capsid immunity).  
Tox: NHP GLP studies at clinically relevant doses showed no toxicity for AAV9.U7snRNA; however high-dose systemic AAV in DMD carries the known hepatic/complement/cardiac risk profile and pre-existing-antibody exclusion.  
Access: Academic origin (Nationwide Children's) - licensable, and attractive because a multi-cassette U7 vector can carry two antisense sequences, which is exactly what a frame-correcting 54-55 or 55-56 double skip needs. Build cost and CMC are much higher than an ASO; one-shot dosing with no titration is a real risk for a first-in-class exon.

**Integrin alpha-v-beta-6 peptide-ligand platform (Arrowhead TRiM) - adjacent, not yet an exon-skipping platform** — Short peptide ligand binding avb6 integrin on myofibres plus lipid modifier; to date conjugated to siRNA (ARO-DUX4, ARO-DM1), not to splice-switching oligos.  
Reach: Skeletal muscle in humans (programs in clinic); deep durable knockdown in NHP skeletal muscle lasting at least 3 months after IV dosing. Cardiomyocyte targeting is a separate, still-preclinical Arrowhead tissue.  
Human PD: none found for exon skipping with this ligand (searched Arrowhead TRiM muscle DMD exon skipping 2026).  
Dose: IV; NHP durability >=3 months supports quarterly-type intervals if it translates.  
Tox: Not established for an oligo-splice payload; no DMD human data.  
Access: Arrowhead partners selectively; this is a watch item, not a 2026-27 build option for exon 55.

**Exon-55-specific clinical precedent** — n/a  
Reach: n/a  
Human PD: none found. Searches run: ClinicalTrials.gov DMD exon 55 (only hit: PBGENE-DMD ARCUS editing, NCT07429240); PubMed 'DMD exon 55 skipping antisense'; PubMed 'double exon skipping single ASO 54 55 56'; PubMed 'exon 55 PMO cocktail 54-55'.  
Dose: n/a  
Tox: n/a  
Access: Open field - no competitor asset to license or be blocked by; also no de-risking data. A nonsense mutation in exon 55 additionally means in-exon PTC removal has a second, non-skipping option space (readthrough/editing) worth a separate assessment.

Unconjugated baseline: Best unconjugated baseline is a dual-ASO naked PMO, not a single ASO: brogidirsen (two PMOs to one exon, exon 44), IV 40 and 80 mg/kg weekly, gave dystrophin 16.63% and 24.47% of normal at 24 weeks (dose-escalation cohorts 10.27% and 15.79%), no SAEs, no discontinuations. Single-ASO naked PMO ceiling: viltolarsen 80 mg/kg weekly = 5.7-5.9% of normal at week 25; golodirsen 30 mg/kg weekly = 1.019% at week 48; casimersen 30 mg/kg weekly = 1.7% vs 0.8% placebo at week 48; eteplirsen 30 mg/kg weekly = 0.93% at 180 weeks. All weekly IV, lifelong, with essentially no cardiomyocyte delivery - the baseline buys skeletal-muscle dystrophin only.

Payload vs delivery: Delivery-driven: Dyne's 3.71-5.46% vs eteplirsen's 0.3-0.9% on the same exon 51 sequence target, and Avidity's ~200 nM muscle PMO vs peptide conjugates. Amenability-driven: exon 44's 4-7% pre-treatment dystrophin (natural skipping) inflates del-zota's 32% and ENTR-601-44's numbers. Potency-driven: brogidirsen's 24% comes from dual-site ASO design plus 80 mg/kg, not delivery. Exon 55 needs a two-exon skip - expect none of these magnitudes to transfer.
