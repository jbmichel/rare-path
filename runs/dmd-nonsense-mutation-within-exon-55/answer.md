# DMD nonsense mutation within exon 55

**Verdict: TEST FIRST**

## Recommendation

**LEAD — Δ54-55 dual-PMO product (exon-54 + exon-55 arms): validate naked, carry forward as anti-TfR1 Fab-PMO conjugates for cardiac coverage.**

Exon 55 is 190 bp (frame remainder 1), so single-exon skipping is non-productive; Δ54-55 is the minimal frame-restoring product (345 bp, -115 aa) with published PMO leads for both exons. TEST FIRST: no coordinated two-exon skip of this block has been quantified and both single-exon intermediates are out of frame.

## Why this design

- Junction fuses R21 2580-2624 to R22 2740-2802: in-phase 108-aa hybrid repeat; cysteine-rich, WW, C-terminal and Dp71 intact.
- 3' endpoint sits inside the empirically mild exon ≤55 interval; smallest in-frame fix, fewest oligos.
- One exon-54 AO reportedly co-skips 54+55: single-oligo upside, run as a probe arm, not the plan.

## Delivery

Left-ventricular exposure decides outcome and naked PMO barely reaches cardiomyocytes, so lead delivery is an anti-TfR1 conjugate; Dyne's DYNE-255 exon-55 Fab-PMO is IND-enabling, so sequence and tox package overlap and partnering beats rebuilding. Avidity/Novartis AOC benchmarks exposure but is not in-licensable. Naked dual PMO on a weekly brogidirsen-like schedule is the fallback and fastest IND, accepting skeletal-muscle-only benefit. Deprioritise cationic PPMO (hypomagnesaemia, eGFR decline, two clinical failures) and AAV9-U7 dual-cassette (non-redosable, non-titratable).

## Backup

- **Δ52-55 four-PMO cocktail.** Only option with in vivo rescue (Dmd Δ52-55 mouse: WT-like truncated dystrophin, normal sarcolemmal DGC, mild late deficit) and an exactly in-phase 103-aa hybrid repeat; cost is four simultaneous skips with out-of-frame intermediates.
- **Δ55-56 two-PMO cocktail.** Equally minimal and exon 56 is a natural multi-exon-skipping acceptor, but no human exon-56 ASO was retrieved, the junction breaks R21 helix C, and the endpoint crosses beyond exon 55.

## Critical risk

Partial skipping is not partial benefit: unless both exons leave the same transcript there is no protein, only added NMD substrate. The 54+55 co-skip claim is one unquantified Discussion line; the closest systematic study found no single PMO skipping two exons, and exon 55 sits in the unfavourable 5'Fast-3'Fast intron class. Δ54-55 has no reported human carrier, so functional quality is inferred from repeat phasing. ESSENCE missed its functional endpoint: schedule a functional readout early and do not import brogidirsen, del-zota or z-rostudirsen dystrophin percentages as expectations.

## First experiment

- **Model:** Myotubes from immortalised exon-55-nonsense DMD myoblasts plus an isogenic CRISPR exon-55 PTC knock-in comparator. Confirm in a second patient line before delivery work.
- **Constructs:** Dose matrix: exon-54 arm alone (Ex54_Ac42 walk, 25-31mers), exon-55 arm alone (Ex55_Ac0/H55 nt 1-34), the pair at several ratios, and a connected-sequence molecule. Scrambled and untreated controls; nucleofection and free uptake, triplicate.
- **Primary readout:** Long-read amplicon sequencing across exons 50-60 for product distribution (Δ54-55 vs single-exon, cryptic, intron-retained), junction RT-ddPCR, NMD ± translation inhibitor, C-terminal-antibody western. Sanger-verify the ACAGAAACCAAG|GACCTCCAAGGT junction.
- **Success:** Δ54-55 dominates skipped species, single-exon skips stay minor, no new cryptic or intron-retained species above background. Dose-dependent dystrophin of expected size in both lines.
- **Kill:** Single-exon intermediates dominate across the dose and ratio matrix, cryptic or intron-retained products appear, or no protein despite skipped transcript. Then switch to Δ52-55.
- **Then, delivery:** Conjugate validated arms to an anti-TfR1 Fab and dose IV in hDMD mice then NHP, measuring junction copies and dystrophin in left ventricle, diaphragm and quadriceps, with cardiac exposure as go/no-go.

## Key precedents

- Single AO to human exon 54 reportedly removed exons 54+55 — the only coordinated-skip signal here; unquantified, so a probe arm.
- Exons 56+57 and 58+59 double skips each needed one PPMO per exon; no single PMO skipped two — budget two oligos.
- Out-of-phase spectrin junctions: DCM at 26 y vs 36 y in-phase, exon 50-55 deletions 43 y; selects the in-phase hybrid.
- DYNE-255, exon-55 Fab-PMO with in vitro skipping, is IND-enabling: partner for sequence plus cardiac-capable delivery.
- Brogidirsen: one product, two antisense sequences, 24.47% dystrophin at 80 mg/kg, 5-year safety — regulatory precedent.
